How to study / accessibility

Each bubble reveals independently. Previous and Next preserve your reveals during this visit. Reset slide hides its bubbles. Large / scroll enlarges the original slide for small screens. Pinch zoom is also available.

Keyboard: Left/Right arrows change slide, Home/End go to first/last, A reveals all on this slide, R resets this slide. Tab to a bubble and press Enter or Space.

The source callouts are flattened images. Their original lettering is revealed without transcription. Bubble controls are keyboard and screen-reader operable, but image-only answer text is not available as a verified screen-reader transcript. Other colored callouts and slides without blue callouts remain visible.
